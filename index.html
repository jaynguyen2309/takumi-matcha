Mixing Instructions

Five steps from bag to bench

  1. 1

    Add 3.5L of cold water to an empty bucket.

  2. 2

    Add the 1.5kg matcha soft serve mix.

  3. 3

    Mix thoroughly until fully dissolved.

  4. 4

    Rest for 4–6 hours — best overnight.

  5. 5

    Pour into the soft serve machine and serve.
